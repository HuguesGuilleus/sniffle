# European Court of Auditors

## Publications

- Official report https://www.eca.europa.eu/en/multiple-reports
  - **Annual reports**: reports about EU budget
  - **Specific annual reports**: reports about EU agencies, decentralised bodies
    and joint undertakings
  - **Special reports**: performance and compliance audits of various specific
    spending and policy areas
  - **Reviews** description and analysis of a topical EU policy area or issue
  - **Opinion** like reviews ask by other UE institution
- **Activity reports**: activity of the ECA
  https://www.eca.europa.eu/en/annual-activity-reports
- **journal**: unofficial periodical
  https://www.eca.europa.eu/en/annual-activity-reports

- https://www.eca.europa.eu/fr/newsletters
